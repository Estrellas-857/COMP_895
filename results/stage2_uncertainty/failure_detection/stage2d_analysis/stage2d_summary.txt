Interpretation
--------------
1. Both MSP uncertainty and predictive entropy detected incorrect pixels better than random ranking in all four adverse conditions.
2. Failure-detection AUROC was strongest for rain and fog and weaker for snow and night.
3. Night had the lowest AUROC despite showing the largest aggregate uncertainty increase in Stage 2C. This indicates that higher global uncertainty does not necessarily imply better pixel-level failure discrimination.
4. Predictive entropy had a small numerical AUROC advantage over MSP in all conditions, but the differences were small.
5. AUPRC must be interpreted relative to the condition-specific error prevalence because the positive-class rate differs substantially across conditions.
