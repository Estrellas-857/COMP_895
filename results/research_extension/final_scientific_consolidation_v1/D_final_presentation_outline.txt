Presentation-wide conventions
Use percentages for ECE, accuracy, coverage and risk, and percentage points for their differences. NLL is in nats per valid pixel. Name the population in every results title, caption or footer. Pool counts and calibration-bin statistics before division; do not label these values as averages over images. Full Night validation has 106 adverse images. The extension has 53 pairs, meaning 53 Night and 53 Normal images; its total of 106 images is a different population.
Canonical semantic predictions remain fixed. Distinguish NLL, ECE, error ordering, and accepted risk. A lower risk at a fixed uncertainty cutoff can result from lower coverage. Stage 5B curves use all valid pixels summarized in 1,000 score bins; the extension uses weighted samples and estimates AURC. Neither estimator should be described as exact unquantized full-resolution ranking. Do not imply significance or independent pixel evidence.
