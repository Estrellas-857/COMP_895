METRIC AND POPULATION DICTIONARY

Populations. Full adverse validation contains 406 images, including 106 Night images. Annotated paired validation contains 203 pairs across the four conditions, including 53 Normal–Night pairs. Original temperature fitting uses 802 adverse training images; threshold selection uses 798 different training images. Controlled calibration transfer uses 101 matched Normal–Night training pairs. These populations and counts are not interchangeable.

mIoU. The union-defined mIoU averages class IoUs where the class union is nonzero. Ground-truth-supported mIoU averages classes with positive ground-truth support. Full Night has 22.3781% under the union definition and 23.6214% under the ground-truth-supported definition. These are different definitions, not inconsistent calculations. The main report should use and label the historical union definition.

Semantic validity and ambiguity. Valid semantic labels are train IDs 0–18; 255 is ignored. ACDC invIds 0/1 provide additional clear/ambiguous annotations and do not replace semantic validity. All-image-pixel coverage includes ignored semantic pixels; valid-pixel coverage excludes them. Region-specific semantic metrics intersect the relevant region with the valid-label mask.

Accepted risk and high confidence. Accepted risk is incorrect accepted valid pixels divided by all accepted valid pixels. High confidence means maximum class probability at least 0.90. The error rate among high-confidence predictions divides incorrect high-confidence pixels by all high-confidence valid pixels. The fraction of errors that are high confidence divides the same numerator by all incorrect valid pixels. Dividing by all valid pixels defines a third quantity. Captions must identify the denominator.

Exact counts and approximate ranking. Stage 5A and extension fixed-threshold statistics use exact counts over full-resolution output pixels. Stages 2 and 3D approximate AUROC and AP using 1,000 uncertainty bins. Stage 5B forms risk–coverage curves from 1,000-bin histograms of all valid pixels and does not report AURC. The extension uses a shared sample of up to 8,192 valid pixels per image, weights N_valid / n_sample, and estimates AURC by summing coverage increments multiplied by cumulative risk. No exact full-resolution AURC is claimed.

Temperature and ranking. A positive scalar temperature preserves the mathematical class argmax. Across different pixels, however, the ordering of multiclass maximum probabilities can change because all competing logits affect softmax. Observed ranking differences also reflect numerical precision, binning, and ties. Do not claim that scalar temperature universally preserves error ranking.

Units and aggregation. Multiply fractions by 100 to obtain percentages; differences between percentages are percentage points. NLL uses natural logarithms and is reported in nats. Pooled pixel metrics and equally weighted image means answer different questions. GPU allocated memory differs from reserved or total GPU memory; MiB and GiB use binary units. Forward-only timing, timing with CPU transfers, and complete application latency use different boundaries.

UNRESOLVED BOUNDARIES

Filenames alone cannot establish independent recordings, drives, or geographic scenes. Treating pixels as independent observations does not justify confidence intervals or significance claims. Exact population ranking cannot be recovered from binned or sampled curves. These limits constrain generalization and fine comparisons while leaving the scoped descriptive conclusions intact.
