STAGE 1 — BASELINE SEGMENTATION

1. Research objective
Measure how the fixed Cityscapes-fine-tuned SegFormer-B0 performs on each adverse ACDC condition, compare corresponding Normal/adverse scenes, and describe major semantic confusions. This establishes the segmentation problem before probability calibration or rejection.

2. Dataset and experimental population
Full adverse validation has Fog 100, Rain 100, Snow 100, Night 106: 406 images. Source per_image_results.csv row counts independently total 406. Semantic-valid pixels by condition, recomputed from stored confusion matrices: 196,154,002; 196,479,419; 195,643,365; 202,435,226. TrainIds 0–18 are evaluated; 255 is ignored.
The separate paired subset contains 50/50/50/53 annotated Normal/adverse pairs: 203 pairs, each with two separately annotated GT maps. All 203 Stage 1 pair identifiers match Stage 2 paired identifiers. Full Night 106 and paired Night 53 must never be treated as the same evaluation population. The 53 Night pairs come from GOPR0351 (28) and GOPR0356 (25).
In the Night paired subset, valid pixels are 107,751,440 Normal versus 101,140,834 adverse. Their masks and scene contents differ. Correspondence permits image-level/scene-level comparison; no exact pixel alignment is assumed. No Normal training labels were used for these validation baselines.

3. Methodology
Frozen segmentation model; no ACDC fine-tuning. Model input 512 × 1024; native logits are bilinearly resized to each image's GT shape with align_corners=False; canonical prediction is unscaled-logit argmax. Confusion rows are GT and columns predicted. Dataset-level mIoU and accuracy are computed from pooled confusion counts, not from equal-image mean metrics. Paired images each use their own GT.

5. Evaluation metrics
mIoU_union averages classes with nonzero predicted/GT union. mIoU_gt separately excludes classes absent from GT. Pixel accuracy is pooled correct/valid pixels. Per-class IoU and GT support are descriptive. Per-image accuracy and latency are saved separately. The historical latency boundaries surround the synchronized model forward only; do not compare them directly to complete Stage 5 pipeline latency.
Night's reported full mIoU_union is 22.3781%, while its alternative mIoU_gt is 23.6214%. GT bus support is zero yet predictions create a nonzero union, so that zero-IoU class contributes to mIoU_union. These are different class-denominator conventions, not an improvement.

8. Limitations and qualifications
These are observational fixed-model results, not causal isolation of weather or proof of safety. One backbone and one dataset limit generality. Normal and adverse frames have differing geometry/content/labels. Paired Night has two source sequence IDs, not 53 independent recordings. Validation informed the exploratory class/failure choices; their examples are not an untouched test or unbiased prevalence sample. The absolute mIoU values depend on union-versus-GT-supported class conventions. Class-support CSVs count true classes on validation and cannot justify training-only predicted-class threshold selection.

STAGE 2 — UNCERTAINTY AND FAILURE DETECTION

1. Research objective
Determine whether raw model uncertainty tracks incorrect predictions and characterize errors that remain highly confident. This motivates calibration while establishing that uncertainty magnitude, error ranking, and confidence correctness are distinct properties.

2. Dataset and experimental population
Full adverse validation: same 406 RGBs and semantic-valid masks as Stage 1. Paired uncertainty: same 203 annotated validation pairs, including 53 Normal–Night pairs, independently verified by CSV identifiers. Targeted audit: two specific Stage 1-selected frames only (Night sky and Snow terrain). All values in the full-condition uncertainty and ROC/AP tables concern full 106 Night images, not the later paired 53 extension.

3. Methodology
Resize logits to GT resolution before raw softmax. MSP uncertainty is 1−maximum softmax probability; normalized predictive entropy is −sum(p log(max(p, 1e−12)))/log(19). Correct/incorrect masks exclude semantic ignore 255. Historical Stage 2 predicts via float32 softmax argmax, which can differ by a single tie pixel from canonical logit argmax.
Distributions are accumulated in 1,000 equal-width bins on[0,1]. Error detection labels incorrect pixels positive and accumulates bins from high to low uncertainty. ROC area uses trapezoidal integration. AUPRC uses sum(delta recall * precision), an average-precision-style step area, not trapezoidal PR area. The calculations approximate the underlying full-resolution score ordering by histogram groups, not a random pixel sample.
Paired analysis computes pixel-weighted global means and separately equal-image pair differences/medians; no calibrated Normal reliability was evaluated in Stage 2.

4. Important configuration parameters
Raw T=1; no model training or fitted uncertainty method. Confidence>=0.90 defines high confidence. There are 19 classes, ignore 255, normalized entropy, 1,000 histogram bins. Representative frames are explicitly frozen in source as Night GOPR0356_frame_000363/sky 10 and Snow GOPR0122_frame_000364/terrain 9. These are case descriptions, not an optimized detector.

5. Evaluation metrics and denominators
AUROC uses incorrect-positive/correct-negative prevalence-independent ROC coordinates. AP/AUPRC depends on condition-specific error prevalence; compare each AP with its own prevalence rather than comparing absolute AP across unequal-error conditions. Mean uncertainty gaps describe score separation but do not establish ranking or calibration.
Full-condition high_conf_wrong_fraction = number of raw confidence>=0.90 incorrect valid pixels / all incorrect valid pixels. It is NOT error rate among high-confidence pixels, NOT percentage of all image pixels, and NOT uncertainty itself. In the targeted audit, high_conf_missed_fraction conditions further on missed GT target-class pixels. Pair CSVs preserve each image's high-confidence-wrong fraction, so averaging those does not reconstruct a pooled high-confidence conditional probability without the denominators.
Histogram-based median/P90 quantities are approximate bin estimates. There are no ECE, NLL, calibrated-temperature comparison, accepted-risk policy, or AURC conclusions attributable to Stage 2 itself.

8. Inconsistencies, limitations, and resolved scope boundaries
Full Rain and Snow confusion matrices differ from Stage 1 by exactly one prediction each (matrix L1 difference 2); Fog and Night match. Explicit raw-softmax tie audits document Rain GOPR0402_frame_000384 at (856, 1347) and Snow GOPR0122_frame_000099 at (335, 1784). These are numerical argmax differences, not learned improvement. Paired Rain adverse and paired Night Normal also have matrix L1 difference 2; other paired-side matrices match. The paired Normal discrepancy is consistent with the differing argmax implementations, but its exact pixel was not audited here. Preserve original results and use later canonical comparisons where exact parity matters; do not silently rewrite old numbers.
AUROC/AP derive from 1,000-bin histograms; small score/tie changes below bin resolution cannot be resolved. Pixels and neighboring frames are correlated. No calibration conclusion follows solely from average uncertainty or high-confidence-error count. The selected cases are post-inspection validation demonstrations. Semantic 255-ignore is separate from ACDC ambiguous-region annotations; Stage 2 does not replace the semantic mask with an ambiguity mask.
No public Normal test labels or test result is implied. No Night+Rain/Night+Snow combined condition was evaluated. No class-aware threshold policy exists in Stage 2. Existing paired raw results should be presented once as motivation, not sold as a new extension.

SUPPORTED CLAIMS FOR THE FINAL NARRATIVE
RQ1 central: Night is hardest for this fixed model on the full adverse validation, and corresponding annotated Normal/Night scenes show a large paired accuracy/mIoU decrease. Supported with population, sequence, and observational qualifications.
RQ2 central: MSP and entropy detect some errors but miss many confident semantic failures; entropy's small ranking edge is descriptive. Supported by full-condition histogram AUROC/AP and explicit conditional high-confidence counts.
The broader calibration-versus-ranking claim requires later Stage 3/5/extension evidence; Stage 1/2 alone establish the failure and imperfect-uncertainty motivation. Do not imply temperature scaling repaired segmentation.
No essential additional inference or experiment is needed for these claims. Formatting existing figures and combining saved numerical records is sufficient. Detailed class heatmaps, all-condition PR curves, paired mean/median variants, and Snow bus examples can move to the appendix. Unsupported causal, safety, deployment-generalization, class-policy, and untouched-validation claims should be weakened or removed.
